The Reinvention of Informality in Peru

Essay · Working draft

Replace this draft with the final version of the essay you submitted to the competition.

The question

Informality is often described as what remains outside the formal economy. Digital platforms and social media complicate that picture: an informal worker can operate with a storefront, audience, payment infrastructure and reputation system without necessarily becoming a formal firm.

Working idea

This essay explores whether technology is merely digitising informality or changing its economic logic — lowering search costs, reshaping customer acquisition and creating new forms of visibility and dependence.
