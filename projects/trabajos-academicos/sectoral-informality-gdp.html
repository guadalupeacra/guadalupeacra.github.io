Sectoral Informality & GDP in Peru

Academic project

Question

How is labour informality associated with sectoral economic performance in Peru?

Approach

Constructed a sector-level panel for Peru and developed an empirical framework for analysing the relationship between informality and sectoral output.

Tools

Panel data · Peruvian official statistics · econometrics · R / Stata

Add the research paper, code and robustness results when the project is ready for public release.
