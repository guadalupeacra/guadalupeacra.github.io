Labour Income & Informality

Question. What factors help explain differences in labour income among workers?

Approach. Cleaned and integrated Peruvian household-survey modules, constructed analytical variables, and estimated regression models.

Output. Reproducible empirical analysis designed to connect data preparation to economic interpretation.

2026 · Panel data

Sectoral Informality & GDP in Peru

Question. How is labour informality associated with sectoral economic performance?

Approach. Constructed a sector-level panel using Peruvian official statistics and developed an empirical framework for the 2010–2025 period.

Output. Research-oriented panel-data project with an emphasis on interpretation and robustness.
