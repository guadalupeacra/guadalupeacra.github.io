2026 Research essay

Power Networks in the Peruvian Insurance Sector: Boards, Banking, and Elites

Co-authored with José Daniel Huamancayo · Economic history

This essay examines the role of insurance companies in Peru’s business networks in the early twentieth century. Using advertisements published in Mundial and Variedades between 1920 and 1924, it reconstructs corporate boards and identifies connections with banking, state fiscal concessions, politics, the press and Lima’s immigrant communities. A comparison of Rimac and Italia reveals two distinct patterns of business networks.
