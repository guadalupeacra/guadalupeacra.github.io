The Reinvention of Informality in Peru

Economics / Society

Draft article · 2026

The question

Informality is often described as what remains outside the formal economy. But digital platforms and social media complicate that picture: an informal worker can now operate with a storefront, audience, payment infrastructure and reputation system without ever becoming a formal firm.

Working idea

This essay explores whether technology is merely digitising informality or changing its economic logic — reducing search costs, reshaping customer acquisition and creating new forms of visibility and dependence.

Why I care about it

The question sits at the intersection of labour economics, entrepreneurship and development: not only why workers are informal, but how they create economic lives within that constraint.
