Microeconomics II

Dynamic games, repeated games, subgame-perfect Nash equilibrium and incentive mechanisms.

Core topics

Dynamic games are best understood by solving from the end of the game backward. The central question is not simply whether a strategy profile is a Nash equilibrium, but whether the prescribed strategies remain optimal in every relevant subgame.

Main concepts

Backward induction · subgame-perfect Nash equilibrium · repeated games · trigger strategies · discount factors.
