Mathematical Economics

Constrained optimisation, convexity, KKT conditions and economic interpretation.

General logic

Constrained optimisation studies the best feasible choice given a set of equality and inequality restrictions. KKT conditions organise the logic of interior and boundary solutions under the appropriate regularity assumptions.

Economic interpretation

The multiplier associated with a constraint can be interpreted as the marginal value of relaxing that constraint, subject to the assumptions of the problem.
