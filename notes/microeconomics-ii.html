Microeconomics II — Dynamic Games

Microeconomics II

Core logic

Dynamic games are best understood from the end of the game backward. The central question is not simply which strategy is a Nash equilibrium, but which strategy remains optimal in every subgame.

Key objects

Subgame-perfect Nash equilibrium · Backward induction · Repeated games · Trigger strategies
