Constrained Optimisation — KKT

Mathematical economics

General model

Consider maximising f(x) subject to equality and inequality constraints. KKT conditions organise the logic of interior and boundary solutions.

Interpretation

The multiplier attached to a constraint measures the marginal value of relaxing that constraint under the regularity conditions of the problem.
